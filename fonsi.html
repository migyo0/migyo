An Assistant
Main Basics
Fonsi lives in your menu bar and sits in the top right or top left of your display once opened. Using two keys, combined with a single tap or double tap, you get four quick actions: Show/Hide, turn on Mic, Mute Fonsi, and Switch Displays (if you have more than one).
Opening apps
Fonsi can open or close multiple apps at once, using "and" or a comma to separate them. This works through text, voice commands, and when building steps for your touch macros.
Hardcoded Commands
Fonsi runs on hardcoded commands to handle things like math equations, definitions, file conversions, wifi speed tests, weather, date and time, and custom commands you create yourself. With AI connected, Fonsi can interpret a vague request and match it to the correct command.
AI Outputs
Fonsi can write out any of his commands in PDF format, and with AI connected, he can write essentially anything into a PDF. He can also generate well-designed HTML files on request. Output quality depends on which API key you're using, ranging from quick answers to more thought-out results.
Touch Macros
The touch feature covers custom commands. Using six steps, you can build touch macros that save a sequence you've created: Open App, New Click, Type Text, Shortcut, Snap Window, and Open URL, covering nearly everything you'd do on a PC. The touch tab also includes an offset setting for macros across multiple displays.
Running Macros
Macros become commands you can trigger from the touch tab directly, or by saying "Run [Touch name]." Timing between each step is fully customizable, so you control the pace of playback.
Onboarding
Startup onboarding sets up the details that help Fonsi understand your commands, including your preferred name, default browser, chosen hotkeys, and general familiarity with how Fonsi works. You can replay onboarding anytime from settings, as mentioned during the process itself.
Settings Page
Settings is where you manage everything set during onboarding, including replaying it. You can add, edit, or remove your API key, switch Fonsi between the top right and top left of your screen, and browse all available commands in the commands tab.
