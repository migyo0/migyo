Daywise 2026

Daywise brings your schedule, tasks, and calendar together in one place, so every part of your day stays visible without piling up. Laid out clean and simple, with nothing competing for your attention. Everything you need, all in one app.

Contact Info

Schedule Page

This is the default start page: a schedule with a clean, gradient-draining design that shows your current block and how much time remains. Colors and icons are fully customizable, and with a collapse and uncollapse slider allowing you to have a focused or broad look at your day.

Detailed Block

Tapping a block opens a sheet showing its description (if one has been added), along with options to edit or delete it. Holding the block brings up the same options. The sheet takes on the color of the block, keeping the look clean and consistent.

Tasks Page

This is the core function of the tasks page, split into three tiers: low, medium, and urgent. Urgent tasks send a daily notification (if notifications are enabled), and each tier is paired with a fitting color and custom icon. If a task has a description, tapping it expands the card to reveal the full text.

Adding Tasks

When creating a task, there's a smooth color fade between each priority level, along with a fluid transition when selecting the "Live" task type. Every level lets you set a title, description, and custom icon, except for Live, which is designed to function as a persistent reminder.

Live Tasks

Adding a Live task turns it into a Live Activity: a constant, visible reminder to complete the task that appears on your home screen, Dynamic Island, and other connected devices, including MacBook, iPad, and CarPlay.

Calendar Page

This is the calendar page, where you can compare events against your schedule seamlessly, and plan ahead easily by creating shared calendars with friends and family.

Event Reminders

The calendar connects smoothly with the schedule page too: events subtly appear on the day picker as small icons. Tapping one opens a sheet detailing the event(s) for that day. This sheet also appears automatically the first time you open the app on a day with a scheduled event.

Profile Page

The profile page offers a snapshot of your overall stats and your friends', showing remaining blocks for the day, upcoming events for the month, and total outstanding tasks.

Friend activity

Daywise's social features go beyond shared calendars. You can also get a glimpse into your friends' day at a glance.

Profile Settings

You can choose, crop, take, or remove your profile picture as you like. Your unique username helps friends find and add you, and your chosen color appears consistently throughout the app.

Colour Palette

The color palette was carefully curated to stay minimal while covering every major and minor color the app needs. Holding a color reveals two complementary variations, with smooth transitions across the entire palette.

Flutter 2026

Flutter is a clean, modern take on the music listening experience. Designed around comfort, clarity, and the needs of the listener. A unique adaptive navigation bar that expands and contracts in response to user interaction.

Contact Info

Home Page

Flutter is an experimental take on the music app. It draws on Spotify and Apple Music, but pushes past their basic presentation with more expressive, dynamic interactions. The home page is built on a stark black and white palette, with a neon lime accent that cuts through it. The contrast keeps the layout clean while giving the app a bold, recognisable identity.

Mini Player

Inspired by Apple's Dynamic Island, the navigation bar carries most of the app's interaction. It pops up into a miniplayer, then expands into a full player, so playback always feels connected to the bar.

Player Expanded

The loop, shuffle and lyrics buttons each have their own small animation. These touches respond to every tap, making the player feel more alive than a set of static icons.

Library Page

In the library, the filter and sort button expands cleanly into its options with a subtle bounce. It stays true to the app's aesthetic while keeping the page uncluttered.

Search Page

Search is where the app's aesthetic is on show. A hero image with a distinct colour contrast sits behind the page, with album covers layered over it to create depth.

Create Playlist

The plus button swings out and turns into a cross as the navigation bar expands into a drawer of playlist options. From there, creating a new playlist is one more step.
